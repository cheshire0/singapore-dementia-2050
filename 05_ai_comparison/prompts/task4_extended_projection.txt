I am working on this research question: How many Singapore residents aged 60 and over will have dementia in 2030 and in 2050, and how many of those cases could be avoided by reducing physical inactivity?

Using the data below:
1. Produce estimates for 2030 and 2050.
2. Estimate how many of those cases could be avoided if the share of older adults who are insufficiently active fell by 15% in relative terms.
3. Break your estimates down by sex, ethnicity, type of dwelling and region.

Explain your method and what drives your results. If you use any figure that is not in the data, say what it is and where it comes from. Answer using only your own knowledge and any data in this message; do not search the web.
